$\gamma_{0}, \gamma_{1}:[a, b]\to \Omega$ — [[knowledge/math/analysis/Путь|Путь]], $\Omega$ — [[knowledge/math/analysis/Область|Область]]

$\gamma_{0}(a)=\gamma_{1}(a)$
$\gamma_{0}(b)=\gamma_{1}(b)$

$\gamma_{0}, \gamma_{1}$ гомотопные пути с неподв. концами, если $\exists \gamma: [a, b]\times[0, 1]\to \Omega$ непр.

$\gamma_{0}(t)=\gamma(t, 0),\ \gamma_{1}(t)=\gamma(t, 1)\ \forall t$
$\gamma(a, u)=\gamma_{0}(a),\ \gamma(b, u)=\gamma_{0}(b)\ \forall u$

## Разъяснение

$\gamma_{u}:[a, b]\to \Omega$ — путь в $\Omega$ c $\gamma_{u}(a)=\gamma_{0}(a),\ \gamma_{u}(b)=\gamma_{0}(b)$
