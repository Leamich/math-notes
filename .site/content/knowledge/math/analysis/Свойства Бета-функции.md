1. $B(p,q)=B(q,p)$
>[!note]- Док-во
> $$
> B(p,q)=\int\limits_{0}^{1} x^{p-1}(1-x)^{q-1}\,dx=[y=1-x]=\int\limits_{1}^{0} (1-y)^{p-1}y^{q-1}(-1)\,dy=B(q, p)
> $$
2. $B(p, q)=\int\limits_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{t^{p-1}}{(1+t)^{p+q}}\,dt$
>[!note]- Док-во
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> B(p, q)=\int\limits_{0}^{1} x^{p-1}(1-x)^{q-1}=\left[ \begin{gathered}
> x=\frac{t}{1+t}=1-\frac{1}{1+t}\\
> dx=\frac{dt}{(1+t)^{2}}\\
> 1-x=\frac{1}{1+t}
> \end{gathered} \right] =\\
> =\int\limits_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{t^{p-1}}{(1+t)^{p-1}}\cdot \frac{1}{(1+t)^{q-1}} \cdot \frac{dt}{(1+t)^{2}}
> \end{gathered}
> $$
