$$
F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
a \ b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ x  \right)=(1-x)^{-a}F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
a \ c-b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ \frac{x}{x-1}  \right)
$$
при $c>b>0,\ a>0,\ \lvert x \rvert, \left\lvert  \frac{x}{x-1}  \right\rvert<1$ (тогда $\lvert x \rvert<\lvert x-1 \rvert$).

(см. [[knowledge/math/analysis/Гипергеометрическая функция|Гипергеометрическая функция]])

## Следствие (формула Эйлера)

$$
F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
a \ b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ x  \right)=(1-x)^{c-a-b}F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
c-a \ c-b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ x  \right)
$$
$c> a+b;a,b>0;\lvert x \rvert<1$

### Док-во

$$
\begin{gathered}
F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
a \ b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ x  \right)\stackrel{\left\lvert  \frac{x}{x-1}  \right\rvert<1 }=(1-x)^{-a}F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
a \ c-b \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ \frac{x}{x-1}  \right)=(1-x)^{-a}F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
c-b \ a \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ \frac{x}{x-1}  \right)=\\
=(1-x)^{-a}\underbrace{ \left( 1-\frac{x}{x-1} \right)^{-c+b} }_{ (1-x)^{c-b} }F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
c-b \ c-a \\ c
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ x  \right)
\end{gathered}
$$

## Док-во

$$
\begin{gathered}
I=(1-x)^{-a}\int\limits_{0}^{1} \frac{t^{b-1}(1-t)^{c-b-1}}{\left( \frac{1}{1-x}-\frac{x}{1-x}t \right)^{a}}\,dt=[s=1-t]=\\
=(1-x)^{-a}\int\limits_{0}^{1}  \frac{s^{c-b-1}(1-s)^{b-1}}{\left( 1-s \frac{x}{x-1} \right)^{a}}\,ds=(1-x)^{-a}F\left( \left. \begin{gathered}
b \ c-b \\ a
\end{gathered}\ \right|\ \frac{x}{x-1}  \right) \cdot \frac{\Gamma(c-b)\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(c)}
\end{gathered}
$$
