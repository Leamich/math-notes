СКО оценки $T$ наз-ся $\mathrm{MSE}_{\theta}(T):=\mathbb{E}_{\theta}[T-\theta]^{2}$.

>[!note]- $\mathrm{MSE}_{\theta}(T)=\mathbb{D}_{\theta}T+b^{2}(\theta)$ (где $b(\theta):=\mathbb{E}_{\theta}T-\theta$)
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> \mathbb{E}_{\theta}[T-\theta]^{2} = \mathbb{E}_{\theta}[(T-\mathbb{E}_{\theta}T)+ \underbrace{ (\mathbb{E}_{\theta}T -\theta) }_{ b(\theta) }]^{2}=\underbrace{ \mathbb{E}_{\theta}[T-\mathbb{E}_{\theta}T]^{2} }_{ \mathbb{D}_{\theta}T } + 2b(\theta) \underbrace{ \mathbb{E}[T-\mathbb{E}T] }_{ =0 } +b^{2}(\theta)=\\
> =\mathbb{D}_{\theta}T+b^{2}(\theta)
> \end{gathered}
> $$
