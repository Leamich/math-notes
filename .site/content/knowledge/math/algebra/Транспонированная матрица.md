$A \in M_{n, m}(K)$, $A=(a_{ij})$

$A^{T} \in M_{m,n}(K): A^{T}=(a_{ji})$.
# Св-ва

1. $(A+B)^{T}=A^{T}+B^{T}$
2. $(cA)^{T}=cA^{T}$
3. $(A^{T})^{T}=A$
4. $(A\cdot B)^{T}=B^{T}\cdot A^{T}$
