1. $0^{*}=0$
2. $Id^{*}=Id$
3. $(\mathscr{A}+\mathscr{B})^{*}=\mathscr{A}^{*}+\mathscr{B}^{*}$
4. $(\lambda \mathscr{A})^{*}=\overline{\lambda}\mathscr{A}^{*}$
5. $\mathscr{A}^{**}=\mathscr{A}$
