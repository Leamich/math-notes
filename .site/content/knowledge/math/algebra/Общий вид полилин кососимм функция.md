$\forall f$ — полилн., кососимм. $\exists c: f=c\cdot \det$ ($c=f(E)$)
# Док-во

* $c\neq 0: \frac{f}{c}(E)=1 \implies \frac{f}{c}=\det$
* $c=0\implies f(E)=0\implies f(e_{\pi(1)},\dots e_{\pi(n)})=0\implies f\equiv 0$
